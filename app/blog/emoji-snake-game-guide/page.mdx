Snake is one of the most iconic browser games ever made, and the Emojar version replaces every pixel with expressive emojis. Your snake is made of emoji characters, the food it eats is an emoji, and the board itself feels alive.

## The Objective

Guide your snake around the grid to eat the food emoji that appears on the board. Each time you eat, your snake grows longer. The game ends if you:

- Run into a wall (the edge of the board), or
- Collide with your own tail.

Your goal is to eat as much food as possible before that happens.

## Controls

| Platform | Move                                  |
| -------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Desktop  | Arrow keys or WASD                    |
| Mobile   | Swipe in the direction you want to go |

The snake moves continuously — you are steering, not stopping.

## Tips for a High Score

- **Plan ahead.** The snake's tail follows the exact path you took, so think two or three moves ahead to avoid trapping yourself.
- **Hug the walls early.** When the snake is short, moving along the edges gives you more open space in the centre.
- **Coil deliberately.** As the snake grows, try to coil in a wide spiral rather than zigzagging — it leaves more room to manoeuvre.
- **Never chase your own tail into a corner.** Corners are where most runs end. Leave yourself an exit.
- **Slow down mentally, not physically.** The game runs at a fixed speed, but you can pause if you need a moment to think.

## Why Emoji Makes It Better

Classic Snake uses simple coloured blocks. Emoji Snake uses characters from the full Unicode emoji set — the food rotates through different fruit and snack emojis each game, and the snake itself has personality. It is the same satisfying loop you remember, just a lot more expressive.
