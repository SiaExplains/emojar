## How to Use It

**Copy a single emoji** — tap any emoji card to instantly copy it to your clipboard.

**Copy the whole pack** — hit "Copy All Emojis" (or "Copy all" on smaller cards) to copy all 10 emojis as a space-separated string, ready to paste into a caption, message, or post.

**See what's coming up** — the page automatically highlights the current or nearest upcoming event so you never have to scroll to find the right pack.

## Seasonal Uses

- **Social media captions** — paste the whole pack into an Instagram or TikTok caption for instant festive flair
- **Chat messages** — drop a themed string of emojis into a group chat to set the mood
- **Email subject lines** — a well-placed 🎃 or 🎄 in a subject line can boost open rates during the holiday season
- **Content calendars** — use the event dates as a seasonal content planning reference
