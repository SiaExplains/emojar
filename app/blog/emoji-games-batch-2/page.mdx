# 8 New Emoji Games: Wordle, 2048, Whack-a-Mole & More

**Published:** 24 June 2026

We just shipped eight brand-new emoji games on Emojar, bringing the total game count to **18 free games** — no download, no account required. Here is what just launched.

---

## 1. Emoji Wordle

[Play Emoji Wordle →](/games/emoji-wordle)

Wordle, but every target word is the official Unicode name of an emoji. You get six guesses to identify a random five-letter emoji name. After each guess the tiles turn green (correct position), yellow (wrong position), or gray (not in the word). A colour-coded on-screen keyboard tracks every letter you have tried. A new random emoji name is picked each game, drawn from the full 3,700+ emoji dataset.

---

## 2. Emoji 2048

[Play Emoji 2048 →](/games/emoji-2048)

The classic 2048 sliding-tile puzzle — but every power-of-two tile is replaced with an emoji. Tiles start as 🌱 (2) and grow through 🌿, 🍀, 🌸, 🌺, 🌻, 🎃, 🎄, 🌈, ⭐, and finally 🏆 (2048). Slide with arrow keys on desktop or swipe on mobile. Your score and personal best are saved in your browser.

---

## 3. Emoji Whack-a-Mole

[Play Whack-a-Mole →](/games/whack-a-mole)

A 3×3 grid of holes with random emojis popping up and hiding. You have 30 seconds to tap or click as many as you can. The spawn interval speeds up over time — what starts at a comfortable pace quickly becomes a frantic reflex test. High scores are stored in localStorage so you can chase your personal best across sessions.

---

## 4. Emoji Speed Sort

[Play Speed Sort →](/games/speed-sort)

One emoji appears at a time and you must press the correct category button — Smileys & Emotion, Animals & Nature, Food & Drink, or Travel & Places — before a 3-second countdown hits zero. Ten rounds per game. Build a consecutive-correct streak for bragging rights. The game reveals the right answer if you miss or run out of time.

---

## 5. Emoji Timeline Challenge

[Play Timeline Challenge →](/games/emoji-timeline)

Five emojis appear in a random scrambled order. Your job is to rearrange them from oldest to newest by their Unicode version number (which is stored in our emoji database for every one of the 3,700+ emojis). Click an emoji to pick it up, then click a slot to swap. Hit Submit to see how many positions you got right. Five rounds, up to 25 total points.

---

## 6. Emoji Pictionary

[Play Emoji Pictionary →](/games/emoji-pictionary)

A curated set of 40 emoji clues, each mapping to one or more valid text answers. You type your guess in a text box and the game auto-accepts the moment you match any valid answer — no submit button required. The 60-second clock keeps ticking, so speed matters as much as knowledge. Skip anything you are stuck on and cycle through as many clues as possible.

---

## 7. Emoji Missing Piece

[Play Missing Piece →](/games/missing-piece)

A 3×3 grid of emojis, all from the same category, with one cell hidden behind a ❓. Pick the correct emoji from four multiple-choice options. Ten rounds, one point per correct answer. The correct answer is always revealed after you pick — right or wrong — so you can learn as you play.

---

## 8. Emoji Story Builder

[Play Story Builder →](/games/story-builder)

Pick one emoji from a set of four options for each of six chapters. After all six chapters are filled, Emojar generates a short narrative sentence for each choice — "Once upon a time, there was a hot beverage…" and so on. When your story is complete you can hit **Share Story** to copy the six-emoji sequence to your clipboard and paste it anywhere.
