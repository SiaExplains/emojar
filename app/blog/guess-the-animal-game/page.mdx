We've added another game to Emojar: **[Guess the Animal](/games/guess-the-animal)**. It's a multiple-choice quiz covering all 130 animal emojis in the Unicode standard — and every round ends with a fun fact about the correct animal.

## How It Works

Each round shows one large animal emoji along with a **category hint** (Mammal, Bird, Reptile, Marine, Amphibian, or Bug). Four animal names appear below it as choices — only one is correct. Click your answer to find out if you were right.

Win or lose, you'll see a **fun fact** about that animal before moving on. After dozens of rounds you'll have quietly learned more about the animal kingdom than you expected.

The game tracks:
- **Correct answers** — your running total for the session
- **Streak** — consecutive correct guesses
- **Best streak** — your personal record
- **Accuracy** — percentage of correct answers overall

A **recent rounds table** shows your last 15 picks so you can review which animals you're missing.

## 130 Animals Across 6 Groups

The quiz covers every animal emoji in Unicode, split across six subgroups:

| Group | Examples |
|-------|---------|
| 🦁 Mammals | Dog, Elephant, Whale, Fox, Otter, Sloth |
| 🐦 Birds | Eagle, Flamingo, Penguin, Parrot, Peacock |
| 🦎 Reptiles | Crocodile, Snake, Turtle, Lizard, Dragon |
| 🐸 Amphibians | Frog |
| 🐠 Marine | Dolphin, Shark, Octopus, Jellyfish, Crab |
| 🐛 Bugs | Butterfly, Honeybee, Ant, Scorpion, Spider |

## Tips

- **Use the category hint.** The subgroup label under the emoji (e.g. "🐠 Marine") immediately rules out entire groups of wrong answers.
- **Similar emojis are the real challenge.** 🐱 Cat Face vs 🐈 Cat, 🐶 Dog Face vs 🐕 Dog — the game includes both, so pay attention.
- **The fun facts are the reward.** Even if you get an answer wrong, you'll leave each round knowing something you didn't before.
