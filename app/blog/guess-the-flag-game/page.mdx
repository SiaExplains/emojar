We've just added a brand-new game to Emojar: **[Guess the Flag](/games/guess-the-flag)**. It's a simple but surprisingly addictive geography quiz built entirely around country flag emojis.

## How It Works

Each round you're shown a large country flag emoji. Four country names appear below it as multiple-choice options — only one is correct. Click your answer to find out if you were right, then move on to the next flag.

The game tracks:
- **Correct answers** — your running total for the session
- **Streak** — consecutive correct guesses in a row
- **Best streak** — your personal best streak so far
- **Accuracy** — percentage of correct answers overall

There's also a **recent rounds table** at the bottom showing your last 15 picks — which flags you got right, which ones tripped you up, and what the right answer was.

## All 259 Country Flags

The quiz covers all **259 country and territory flags** from the Unicode emoji standard. That includes well-known flags like 🇺🇸 🇬🇧 🇯🇵 🇩🇪 as well as smaller territories and island nations that will definitely test even seasoned geography buffs.

Some flags look deceptively similar — Chad 🇹🇩 and Romania 🇷🇴 share almost identical vertical stripes, and a handful of African and Asian flags will challenge even frequent travellers. Part of the fun is discovering just how many flags you didn't know you knew (or didn't know you didn't know).

## Why Flag Emojis?

Flag emojis occupy a unique corner of the emoji world. Unlike face or object emojis, every flag emoji encodes a specific ISO 3166 country code — two regional indicator letters that combine to form the flag. They're one of the more technically interesting emoji families, and they're perfect for a quiz format.

We've been looking for a way to surface the Flags category more prominently on Emojar, and this game felt like a natural fit.
