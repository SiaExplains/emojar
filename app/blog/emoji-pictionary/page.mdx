Pictionary in the real world involves drawing. In Emoji Pictionary the picture is already provided — as a text clue — and your job is to produce the emoji that matches it. It sounds like it should be easy. "Yellow fruit that monkeys love" is obviously 🍌. But clues get more cryptic as the game progresses, and you are always racing a clock.

## How It Works

A text clue appears on screen describing an emoji. Your task: type or select the emoji that fits the description in the answer box. Once you submit a correct answer the next clue immediately appears. There are **40 clues per session** and a **60-second timer** running the whole time.

You do not need to answer them all — your score is the number you get correct before time runs out. A perfect run would mean answering all 40 correctly within 60 seconds, which requires roughly 1.5 seconds per clue. That is extremely fast, and most players aim to clear as many as they can rather than expecting perfection.

## The Skip Option

If a clue has you completely stumped, you can skip it. The clue moves to the bottom of the queue and a new one appears immediately. Skipping costs no points — only time. A good strategy is to skip anything that does not come to you within two seconds and return to it if time allows. Burning five seconds on one clue is worse than moving on and answering three easy ones.

## Example Clues

The clues range from literal to playful:

- "Yellow fruit that monkeys love" → 🍌
- "The emotion you feel when laughing so hard you cry" → 😂
- "A frozen weather event, the kind that cancels school" → ❄️
- "What you might find under the sea, eight arms, ink" → 🐙
- "A classic two-player board game, black and white discs" → ♟️

Harder clues describe emoji by metaphor, cultural reference, or an indirect property rather than a direct description. These are the ones the skip button is for.

## Tips for a High Score

**Use your emoji keyboard's search.** On mobile devices, the emoji keyboard has a search function — typing "pizza" shows 🍕 immediately. If you can type the keyword faster than browsing, the search shortcut saves vital seconds.

**Think broadly for abstract clues.** A clue describing "a powerful and dangerous natural event at sea" could be 🌊 or 🌀 — if one does not work, try the other before skipping.

**The obvious answer is usually right.** Emoji Pictionary is not trying to trick you with lateral thinking puzzles. If your first instinct is a specific emoji, go with it.

**Practice emoji input speed.** The bottleneck for most players is not knowing the answer — it is getting the emoji onto the screen fast enough. Familiarity with your platform's emoji picker or keyboard shortcut (Windows: Win+. / Mac: Ctrl+Cmd+Space) directly improves your score.
