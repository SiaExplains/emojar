The key length conversions are exact: **1 inch = 2.54 cm**, **1 foot = 30.48 cm**, and **1 mile = 1.609344 km**. Going the other way, 1 km is about 0.6214 miles and 1 meter is about 3.281 feet. Since 1959, imperial units have been officially defined in metric terms, so these factors aren't approximations; any rounding you see comes from the decimals you choose to keep.

Below are eight situations where people regularly need a length conversion, with the numbers worked out.

## Exact factors worth remembering

| From           | To          | Multiply by      |
| -------------- | ----------- | ---------------- |
| inches         | centimeters | 2.54 (exact)     |
| feet           | meters      | 0.3048 (exact)   |
| yards          | meters      | 0.9144 (exact)   |
| miles          | kilometers  | 1.609344 (exact) |
| nautical miles | meters      | 1,852 (exact)    |

## 1. Running race distances

Race distances are set in kilometers almost everywhere, while many runners track their pace in miles.

- 5K = **3.11 miles** (3.106855961)
- 10K = **6.21 miles**
- Marathon, 42.195 km = **26.22 miles** (26.21875746)

That last figure is why the marathon is usually quoted as "26.2 miles".

## 2. Height in feet and inches

Height is the conversion people most often get wrong, because 5 ft 9 in is **not** 5.9 feet. There are 12 inches in a foot, so 9 inches is 0.75 of a foot: 5 ft 9 in = 5.75 ft.

Simplest method: convert everything to inches first. 5 × 12 + 9 = 69 inches, and 69 × 2.54 = **175.26 cm**. A height of 6 ft exactly is **1.8288 m**.

## 3. TV and monitor sizes

Screens are sold by their diagonal in inches, even in metric countries. A 65-inch TV has a diagonal of 65 × 2.54 = **165.1 cm**. That is the diagonal, not the width, so check the width separately before you buy a stand.

## 4. Bicycle wheels and tire sizes

A wheel listed as 27 inches is about **68.58 cm** across. Wheel sizes are traditional names rather than precise measurements, so for tires, rely on the metric ISO size printed on the sidewall.

## 5. Sports fields

An American football field is 100 yards between the goal lines, which is **91.44 m**. Most track events are measured in meters, so a 100 m sprint is a little longer than 100 yards.

## 6. Boating and flying

Ships and aircraft measure distance in **nautical miles**: 1 nmi = 1,852 m exactly, or about **1.1508 statute miles**. A nautical mile was originally based on one minute of latitude, which is why it doesn't line up neatly with the land mile.

## 7. Small measurements

In engineering and DIY, millimeters and fractions of an inch meet. Half a millimeter is about **0.0197 inches**, and a quarter inch is exactly 6.35 mm. For very small scales, the converter also covers micrometers (µm) and nanometers (nm), useful for things like filter ratings or wavelengths of light.

## 8. Road distances and signs

Driving in a country that uses the other system? 1 km is 0.6214 miles, so a sign reading 80 km is about 50 miles away. For speed limits, which are a different quantity, use the [Speed Converter](/online-tool/unit-converters/speed-converter) instead.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating 5'9" as 5.9 feet.** It is 5.75 feet.
- **Confusing nautical and statute miles.** They differ by about 15%.
- **Converting area with length factors.** A square foot is not 30.48 cm²; it is 30.48 × 30.48 = 929.0304 cm². For floor plans and land, use the [Area Converter](/online-tool/unit-converters/area-converter).

## FAQ

### Why is a mile 1.609344 km and not a rounder number?

The mile is defined as 5,280 feet, and the foot as exactly 0.3048 m. Multiply them and you get 1,609.344 m.

### How many feet is a meter?

About 3.2808 feet, or 3 feet and roughly 3.37 inches. One meter is 39.37 inches.

### Are US and UK inches the same?

Yes. Since the 1959 international agreement, the inch, foot, yard and mile have had the same length in both countries.
