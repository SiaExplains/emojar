To plan for a baby with a calculator, start from the first day of your last period. The [Ovulation Calculator](/online-tool/calculators/ovulation-calculator) estimates your fertile days from that date and your cycle length, and the [Pregnancy Due Date Calculator](/online-tool/calculators/due-date-calculator) turns the same date into a due date and a list of milestones. Once you have a due date you can count back to work deadlines, set a monthly savings figure and see how old an older child will be when the baby arrives. Every date here is a calendar estimate and none of it is medical advice, so your care provider's dates win if they differ.

The example is a couple thinking about a second child. Their first was born on August 19, 2024. The last period started on Monday, February 1, 2027, and cycles run about 30 days. They want to know when to try, when a baby might come, what to tell work and when, and whether they can put 6,000 aside in time.

## When is the fertile window in a 30-day cycle?

Open the Ovulation Calculator and enter February 1, 2027 as **First day of last period** and 30 as **Average cycle length**. Leave **Luteal phase length** at 14 unless you track it. The results:

- **Estimated ovulation:** Wednesday, February 17, 2027
- **Most fertile days:** February 15 to February 17
- **Fertile window:** February 12 to February 18
- **Next period expected:** Wednesday, March 3
- **Pregnancy test from:** Thursday, March 4

Ovulation lands 16 days after the period starts because the tool counts back from the next period. It takes the cycle length minus the luteal phase, so a 28-day cycle gives 14 days and a 30-day cycle gives 16. The fertile window is the five days before ovulation plus the day after.

Open **Next three cycles** under the results to look further ahead. If February doesn't work out, the next windows are March 14 to 20 and April 13 to 19. Cycle length has to be between 20 and 45 days, and the luteal phase between 9 and 18.

## How do you work out a due date from your last period?

In the Pregnancy Due Date Calculator, set **Calculate from** to **First day of last period**, enter February 1, 2027 in **Date** and 30 in **Average cycle length**. The tool adds 280 days, plus two more because the cycle is two days longer than 28. That gives an **Estimated due date** of Wednesday, November 10, 2027.

If you're trying out a date that hasn't happened yet, the calculator stops with "That date is in the future relative to “today”." To get past that, change the **Today** field, which sets the day used for "how far along". With **Today** set to March 15, 2027, this example reads 5 weeks 5 days (first trimester), with 240 days until the due date and an estimated conception date of February 17. That matches the ovulation estimate above.

If you know the conception date, choose **Conception date** and the tool adds 266 days. February 17 gives the same November 10. There are also options for **IVF transfer (3-day embryo)** at 263 days and **IVF transfer (5-day embryo)** at 261 days.

## How do you count back from the due date for work notice and leave?

The [Date Calculator](/online-tool/calculators/date-calculator) handles this. Set **Calculation** to **Subtract from a date**, put November 10, 2027 in **Start date**, and enter the gap in **Years**, **Months**, **Weeks** and **Days**. The **Days** field starts at 30, so set it to 0 if you only want weeks.

Rules on notice and leave depend on your country and employer, so check yours first and use your own numbers. Suppose your employer wants notice 15 weeks before the due date. Enter 15 in **Weeks** and the **Resulting date** is Wednesday, July 28, 2027. If you plan to stop work four weeks before the due date, entering 4 gives Wednesday, October 13.

To look forward instead, choose **Add to a date**. Six months from November 10, 2027 is Wednesday, May 10, 2028, which is a useful first guess at a return date or the point when a childcare place needs to start.

## What day of the week do those dates land on?

The Date Calculator shows the weekday, but the [Day of the Week Calculator](/online-tool/date-time-tools/day-of-week-calculator) adds more. Enter October 13, 2027 and it shows a Wednesday in ISO week 41, in Q4. A midweek finish is awkward, so the couple moves the last day to Friday, October 15, which the same tool confirms.

The table called **The same date in other years** is handy for birthdays. Enter November 10, 2027 and it shows that date falling on a Friday in 2028, a Saturday in 2029 and a Sunday in 2030. The jump from Wednesday to Friday happens because 2028 is a leap year.

## How much do you need to save each month before the baby comes?

The [Savings Goal Calculator](/online-tool/calculators/savings-goal-calculator) has no currency setting, so it works with any money. Enter 6000 as **Savings goal**, 1200 as **Saved so far** and 3 as **Interest / return**. Set **Calculate** to **Monthly saving needed**.

The couple starts saving in February and has nine months until November, so **Reach the goal in** is 0.75 years. **Save each month** comes out at 525.02, which adds up to 4,725.20 in deposits, and interest supplies the other 74.80. If they give themselves a full year, the figure drops to 391.53.

To test a monthly amount you already know you can manage, switch to **Time to reach the goal** and enter 300 as **Monthly saving**. That takes 1 year 4 months, with 139.97 in interest along the way. The tool assumes deposits at the end of each month and monthly compounding, and it doesn't adjust for inflation.

## How old will your older child be when the baby arrives?

In the [Age Calculator](/online-tool/calculators/age-calculator), enter August 19, 2024 as **Date of birth** and November 10, 2027 in **Age on**. The **Age** result is 3 years, 2 months, 22 days, or 38 in **Total months**.

Try the next cycle as well. With March 3 as the last period, the due date moves 30 days to December 10, 2027, and the gap becomes 3 years, 3 months, 21 days. Once the baby is born, the same tool gives their exact age for forms and appointments.

All six calculators run in your browser. The dates you type stay on your device and aren't saved or sent anywhere.

## FAQ

### Can I use the Ovulation Calculator as contraception?

No. Cycles vary and ovulation can shift by several days from one cycle to the next, so calendar estimates are not reliable contraception.

### How accurate is the due date?

It's an estimate. The calculator notes that only about 1 in 20 babies arrives on the due date, and most arrive within two weeks either side. An early ultrasound is the most accurate way to date a pregnancy.

### Why does cycle length change the due date?

The standard 280-day count assumes a 28-day cycle. The calculator adds or removes a day for each day your cycle differs, so a 30-day cycle gives 282 days and a 26-day cycle gives 278.

### Does the Date Calculator include the end date?

No. It counts the nights between two dates, like hotel stays. To include both days, use the [Date Difference Calculator](/online-tool/date-time-tools/date-difference-calculator).
