Emoji Kitchen is one of the most creative things to come out of Google's emoji work. It lets you mash any two emojis together into a single hybrid image — a 😂 + 🔥 becomes a laughing flame, a 🐸 + ❤️ becomes a frog holding a heart. Emojar brings Emoji Kitchen directly into the site so you can mix, preview, and download combinations instantly.

## What Is Emoji Kitchen?

Emoji Kitchen is a feature originally built by Google for Gboard (the Google keyboard on Android). Google's designers hand-drew thousands of combination images — each one a unique artistic interpretation of two emojis merged together.

The asset library is publicly accessible, which is what powers the [Emoji Kitchen tool](/emoji-kitchen) on Emojar. All credit for the combination artwork goes to Google and the Noto Emoji team.

## How to Use It

Open the [Emoji Kitchen](/emoji-kitchen) tool and search for your first emoji in the left panel. Then search for your second emoji in the right panel. If a combination exists in the Google library, the mashup image appears instantly in the preview area.

Hit **Download** to save the combination image as a PNG to your device. The downloaded image is the same high-quality asset served from Google's CDN.

## Not Every Combination Exists

Google has drawn combinations for thousands of pairs, but not every possible combination has an image. If you pick two emojis that don't have a dedicated mashup, the tool will let you know. The most reliable combinations involve popular face emojis (😀😍🥺😭) combined with objects, animals, or other faces.

## What People Use It For

**Reaction images**: A custom combo often expresses something more specific than a standard emoji — send a 😤+💪 when you're determined, or 🥺+💧 when you're genuinely emotional.

**Stickers and profile pictures**: The combination images are clean, high-quality illustrations that work well as chat stickers or profile picture overlays.

**Creative content**: Use mashups in blog graphics, slides, or social posts as unique visual elements that stand out from stock emoji.

**Just for fun**: Honestly, discovering unexpected combinations is entertaining on its own.
