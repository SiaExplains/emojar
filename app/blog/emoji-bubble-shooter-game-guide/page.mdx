Bubble Shooter is one of the most satisfying puzzle games ever made — there is nothing quite like watching a whole cluster of bubbles vanish in a chain reaction. The Emojar version replaces flat coloured balls with emoji bubbles, turning every shot into a tiny burst of expression.

## The Objective

Clear as many emoji bubbles from the board as possible by matching three or more of the same emoji in a cluster. The game ends when the bubble stack reaches the bottom of the board.

## How It Works

1. You control a **launcher** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Aim by moving your cursor (or dragging on mobile) and click (or tap) to fire a bubble.
3. When a fired bubble connects with **two or more identical bubbles**, the whole group pops.
4. Any bubbles that were only hanging from the popped group **fall away** as bonus clears.
5. The board descends over time — keep clearing to stay ahead.

The next bubble in the queue is shown beside the launcher, so you can plan two shots ahead.

## Controls

| Platform | Action                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------- |
| Desktop  | Move mouse to aim, click to shoot |
| Mobile   | Drag to aim, tap to shoot         |

## Tips for a High Score

- **Use the walls.** Bubbles bounce off the side walls. A well-placed bank shot can reach clusters that have no direct line of fire.
- **Aim for the neck.** Popping the narrow connection between a large cluster and the ceiling causes the whole dangling group to fall — massive bonus clears.
- **Match the colour shown in the queue.** If the next bubble is one you cannot use effectively right now, consider whether banking it into a corner sets up a better shot later.
- **Do not panic-shoot.** Firing randomly when the board descends speeds up the descent faster than careful, deliberate shots.
- **Chain reactions are everything.** One well-placed shot that triggers a cascade is worth ten single pops. Look for the angle that causes the most domino falls.
- **Keep the centre clear.** Letting bubbles pile up in the middle cuts off wall-bounce opportunities.

## Why Emoji Bubbles?

Standard Bubble Shooter uses uniform solid colours. Emoji Bubble Shooter uses actual emoji characters — each bubble is recognisable, giving the board personality and making matches feel more rewarding. Watching a cluster of 🍉🍉🍉 pop is simply more satisfying than watching three green circles disappear.
