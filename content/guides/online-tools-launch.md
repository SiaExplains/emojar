## Why does "runs in your browser" matter?

Most online converters work by uploading your file to a server, processing it there and sending the result back. That is slow on a bad connection, and it means a copy of your passport scan, contract or holiday photos sits — at least for a while — on somebody else's computer.

Emojar's tools do the work on your own device instead. Modern browsers are surprisingly capable: they can decode and encode images, read and write PDFs, hash files, generate secure random numbers and even run compiled programs through WebAssembly. Emojar simply puts those capabilities behind a friendly interface.

In practice that means:

- **Your files never leave your device.** There is no upload step and nothing to delete afterwards.
- **It is fast.** No upload queue, no waiting for a server — a batch of photos converts in seconds.
- **It keeps working offline.** Once a tool page has loaded, the processing itself does not need an internet connection.

A few tools need to download an engine the first time you use them — for example the OCR engine behind [Image to Text](/online-tool/image-tools/image-to-text). Even then, only the engine is downloaded; your image stays with you. Each tool page says clearly when that applies.
