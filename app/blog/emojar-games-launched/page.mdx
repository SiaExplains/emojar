We're excited to announce the launch of the **Emojar Games** page — a collection of fun, emoji-themed mini-games you can play directly in your browser, no download or account required.

## What's in the Games Gallery?

We launched with five games, each built around emojis to make them feel fresh and playful:

### ❌⭕️ XO Game

The classic tic-tac-toe you know and love, reinvented with emoji symbols. Challenge yourself or play against a friend in the same browser.

### 🐍🍏 Snake Game

Guide your emoji snake around the board, eat the food, and grow as long as you can without hitting the walls or yourself. A timeless classic with an emoji twist.

### 💣🟦 Minesweeper

Uncover emoji tiles and avoid the hidden bombs. The same logic puzzle that has kept people thinking since the 90s — now with emoji flair.

### 🤔🔤 Hangman

Guess the mystery emoji word letter by letter before you run out of tries. A fun way to test your emoji vocabulary.

### 🫧🎯 Bubble Shooter

Match and pop emoji bubbles to clear the board and rack up points. Satisfying, colourful, and just a little bit addictive.

## Why Games on an Emoji Site?

Emojis are expressive and fun — so it felt natural to build games that put them front and centre. These mini-games are lightweight, mobile-friendly, and load instantly, staying true to what Emojar is all about: no friction, just fun.
