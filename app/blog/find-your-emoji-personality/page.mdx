## The Science Behind It

The quiz is rooted in the **Big Five personality model** (also known as OCEAN) — the most widely validated framework in personality psychology, developed over decades of research by psychologists including Lewis Goldberg and Paul Costa. The Big Five describes personality across five broad dimensions:

| Trait | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Openness** | Creativity, curiosity, and willingness to explore new ideas |
| **Conscientiousness** | Organisation, grit, dependability, and goal-directed behaviour |
| **Extraversion** | Social energy, enthusiasm, and positive affect |
| **Agreeableness** | Warmth, empathy, trust, and cooperation |
| **Neuroticism** | Emotional reactivity and tendency toward negative affect (we flip this to **Calm**) |

For Emojar's quiz, we adapted these five dimensions into language that feels natural in the context of emojis: **Joy, Warmth, Calm, Openness, and Grit** (J, W, C, O, G). The labels are different; the underlying science is the same.

## How the Matching Works

Each of the 12 questions has four answer choices. Every choice quietly awards points to one or more personality dimensions based on what psychologists know about behaviour and self-report patterns.

For example, a question about *how you handle a setback* might award high Grit points for "pick yourself up and try again," high Openness for "channel it into something creative," and high Calm for "reflect deeply and extract every lesson."

Once you finish, your answers are summed into a five-dimensional personality vector — your personal fingerprint across J, W, C, O, and G.

We then compare your vector against **30 emoji personalities** using **cosine similarity** — a standard technique from machine learning that measures how closely two vectors point in the same direction. The five emojis whose personality profiles best align with yours become your matches.

## What Makes a Good Emoji Match?

Every emoji in the pool was given a trait vector based on its cultural associations, Unicode description, and how people actually use it. A few examples:

- **🦁 Lion** scores very high on Grit and moderately on Openness — the courageous, independent type.

- **🎯 Direct Hit** maximises Grit and Calm — the focused strategist who cuts straight to what matters.
