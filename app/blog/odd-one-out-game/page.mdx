We've added another game to Emojar: **[Odd One Out](/games/odd-one-out)**. Five emojis appear on screen. Four of them share a common theme — and one doesn't fit. Your job is to find it.

## How It Works

Each round shows **five emojis** in a row. Four belong together and one is the intruder. Tap the one you think doesn't fit.

After you pick, the game reveals:
- Whether you were **correct or not**
- The **theme** the four emojis share
- A plain-English **explanation** of why the odd one out doesn't belong

Then hit **Next Round** to keep going. The emojis within each round are shuffled so the odd one out isn't always in the same position.

The game tracks:
- **Correct answers** — your total for the session
- **Round number** — which round you're on out of 50
- **Accuracy** — your percentage of correct picks

After all 50 rounds are played, the deck reshuffles and starts again in a new random order.

## Tips

- **Think in categories first.** Before picking, ask yourself: "What do four of these have in common?" The odd one out becomes obvious once you identify the theme.
- **Tricky rounds go beyond the obvious.** Some rounds involve subtler distinctions — like four *stormy* weather types with one *sunny* day, or four *Italian* foods with one *Mexican* dish.
- **The theme is revealed after every guess** — even if you get it right, reading the explanation often surfaces something you didn't consciously notice.
