## Why download an emoji as a PNG?

Emojis are text characters. When you paste one into a design tool, a presentation, or a print file, the result depends entirely on what font is installed on that device. The same emoji can look completely different on Windows versus Mac versus Android.

A PNG is different — it is a fixed image. It looks identical everywhere, at any size, on any device or platform. That makes it reliable for:

- **Stickers** (Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp) — export with a transparent background for a clean cutout
- **Design mockups** in Figma, Canva, or Photoshop — drop in a crisp 1024×1024 without pixelation
- **Social media** thumbnails, story overlays, and post graphics
- **Presentations** — embed a clean emoji PNG instead of a blurry text character that renders differently on each machine
- **Print and merchandise** — T-shirts, sticker sheets, mugs — any use case that needs a vector-quality raster image

## Tips

**For stickers:** Choose *Transparent* background and *Medium* or *Large* padding. The padding gives the emoji some breathing room when placed on different coloured backgrounds.

**For social media:** Use *White* or a colour-matched background at 512×512 or 1024×1024. A solid background stops the emoji from looking cut-out against the platform's UI.

**For Figma / design tools:** Export at 1024×1024 with *None* or *Small* padding, then scale down inside your design. You get the full detail without committing to a small pixel count early.

**For multiple emojis:** Type or paste a sequence of emojis (or even text combined with emojis) into the custom text box. The tool renders whatever you type, so you can create a row of emojis as a single image.
