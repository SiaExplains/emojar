It has been a busy month at Emojar HQ. Here is a quick rundown of everything that has landed on the site recently.

## 🎮 Emoji Games — Five Mini-Games, All Free

The biggest addition: a full **Emoji Games** section with five browser-playable mini-games, each built around emojis.

- **XO (Tic-Tac-Toe)** — play against a friend or a computer opponent, choose your emoji pieces, and pick from three board sizes.
- **Snake** — guide your emoji snake, eat the food, and survive as long as possible.
- **Minesweeper** — classic mine-clearing logic with an emoji skin.
- **Hangman** — guess the hidden emoji word before you run out of guesses.
- **Bubble Shooter** — pop coloured emoji bubbles to clear the board.

All games are mobile-friendly and load instantly — no download, no account required. Head over to the [Games Gallery](/games) to try them out.

## 🏠 Refreshed Homepage

The homepage got a visual reorder. The **Emoji Games** card now appears right at the top — before the search bar and emoji grid — so new visitors can discover the games without scrolling.

Below that, the **Copy & Paste** section, search, category filters, and popular emojis remain exactly where they were.

## 📂 Category Breadcrumbs

Every category page (for example [Animals & Nature](/category/Animals%20%26%20Nature)) now shows a breadcrumb at the top:

> **Categories** › Animals & Nature

Clicking **Categories** takes you back to the [full categories index](/categories) so you can jump between groups without using the browser back button.

## ℹ️ Improved About Page

The [About page](/about) now fully documents everything Emojar can do — search & copy, category browsing, A–Z sorting, the games, and the blog — so new visitors understand the whole platform at a glance.

## What's Next?

More games are in the pipeline, along with deeper per-emoji pages (history, cross-platform rendering differences, and usage tips). If you have a suggestion, [drop us a note](/contact).

Thanks for using Emojar! 🫙
