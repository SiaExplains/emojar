Need to brand a photo, add a fun overlay, or protect an image before sharing it online? The [Emoji Watermark Tool](/emoji-watermark) on Emojar lets you place any emoji directly on your photo and download the result — no accounts, no uploads to a server, and no watermark on your watermark.

## How to Use It

Open the tool and upload any photo from your device. The image loads into the editor where you can:

- **Choose your emoji** — search any of the 3,790 emojis on Emojar and select one as your watermark
- **Resize** — drag the size slider to make the emoji as subtle or as bold as you want
- **Reposition** — click and drag the emoji anywhere on the image
- **Set opacity** — lower it for a translucent overlay, keep it at 100% for a solid stamp
- **Download** — hit the download button and save the composited image to your device

The whole process takes under a minute.

## Your Photos Never Leave Your Device

This is worth emphasising: the tool runs entirely in your browser using the HTML Canvas API. Your photo is never uploaded to any server. Once you close the tab, the image data is gone — nothing is stored or transmitted.

This makes it suitable for sensitive photos, client work, or any situation where you want image editing without cloud exposure.

## Use Cases

**Social media branding**: Add your logo emoji (or a consistent signature emoji) to photos before posting to Instagram or X/Twitter.

**Meme and reaction images**: Overlay a perfectly placed 💀, 👀, or 🔥 for comedic effect before sharing in group chats.

**Photography credits**: Use a distinctive emoji as a lightweight "signature" to identify your photos if they spread without attribution.

**Event photography**: Stamp event photos with a thematic emoji before distributing them to guests.

## Tips for Best Results

Use a high-contrast emoji against your photo's dominant color — a ⭐ on a dark sky, a 🌿 on a bright background. For a subtle watermark, reduce opacity to around 30–40%. For a corner stamp, position the emoji near the bottom-right and keep it small.
